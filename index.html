About

I build intelligent applications that solve real-world problems, taking them from raw data all the way to production.

I am an aspiring AI/ML Engineer based in London, currently completing an MSc in Data Science at the University of Hertfordshire. My interests lie in Machine Learning, Generative AI, MLOps and cloud deployment. I enjoy taking projects from idea to production, covering data processing, model development, deployment, CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure.

I am currently building end-to-end AI applications, including NLP systems, recommendation engines and MLOps-driven projects such as ScholarAI, a retrieval-augmented research assistant. I believe the best way to learn is by building, which is why I continuously work on practical projects that strengthen both my machine learning and software engineering skills.
